# Human Review Checklist

Check these before you decide whether to try again.

- [ ] The classification rests on what was said, not on the story that would make most sense of it
- [ ] A blended reason wasn't squeezed into one tidy factor. If no stated cause was confirmed as the main one, they stay visibly separate
- [ ] A real no-decision wasn't mistaken for a rejection, or the other way round
- [ ] No reason to try again was invented where the evidence only supports an unknown
- [ ] An unconditional decline was respected, not softened into a reason to try a different angle
