# Post-Mortem: Four Cases

Source: [cases.md](cases.md), fictional.

## Case A: Bramwell Analytics

**Classification: `hard-blocker`.** A structural requirement, five days a week in a specific office, against a confirmed remote-only need, is real and not something either side is likely to change. This is not timing or a soft no.

**What would justify trying again:** a genuinely different, remote-eligible role opening, which the company itself named as a possibility. Not this same role, and not "checking back" without one.

## Case B: Thistlewood Community Fund

**Classification: `timing`.** The board paused all new grants for a stated, specific reason (a strategy review) with a named expected timeframe (Q1 next year), and explicitly invited a reapplication. This is not a rejection of the application's merits at all.

**What would justify trying again:** the stated trigger itself, the strategy review concluding and the next funding cycle opening. Reapplying before then would be ignoring the actual information given.

## Case C: Corvid Print Studio

**Classification: `no-decision`.** A specific concern was raised, a specific answer was sent addressing it directly, and then contact stopped. Nobody said no. This is a live thread nobody returned to, not a quiet decision against it.

**What would justify trying again:** nothing needs to justify it, this needs a follow-up on the answer already sent, not a wait for an external trigger. Treating this the same as Case A or B, and waiting for a "reason" to reappear, would miss that the ball is simply still in their court.

## Case D: Cold Outreach

**Classification: Genuinely over.** This is an explicit, unconditional decline with a direct request not to follow up again. There is no trigger to name here, and this write-up will not suggest one. Treating "not interested in this or anything similar, please don't follow up" as an opening for a different angle later would be ignoring a clear answer.

**What would justify trying again:** nothing. This is closed.

## Summary

Four different outcomes needed four different responses: a genuine structural mismatch that only a different opportunity resolves, a real pause with its own stated timeline, a live thread that just needs a normal follow-up rather than a wait, and a clear no that should stay closed. Treating any of these the same as another would have gotten at least one of them wrong.
