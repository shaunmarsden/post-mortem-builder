# Fictional Cases: Four Failed Attempts

All entirely fictional, built to test different classifications.

## Case A: Job Application, Bramwell Analytics

Applied for a data analyst role. Final message: "Thanks for your time throughout the process. The role requires five days a week in our Manchester office, and you confirmed you need remote-only. We won't be moving forward, but we'd welcome a future application for a remote-eligible role if one opens up."

## Case B: Grant Application, Thistlewood Community Fund

Applied for a small community grant. Final message: "We're not able to fund any new applications this round, our board has paused all new grants pending a strategy review, expected to conclude in Q1 next year. Please do reapply once the next cycle opens."

## Case C: Partnership Pitch, Corvid Print Studio

Pitched a local print studio on a joint referral arrangement. They raised a concern about the extra admin workload on their side. A detailed answer was sent back addressing exactly that, proposing a simplified process needing no extra admin from them. No reply since, six weeks ago.

## Case D: Cold Outreach to a Potential Collaborator

Reached out to a local business owner about a possible collaboration. Reply: "Thanks, but I'm not interested in this or anything similar, please don't follow up again."
