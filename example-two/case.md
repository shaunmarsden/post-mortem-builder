# Fictional Case Two: A Blended Reason

An entirely fictional community theatre audition, testing a harder case than the first four: a stated reason that blends more than one factor together rather than naming one clean cause.

## The Case

Applied for a role in a local production. Final message: "We loved your audition, but we've decided to go a different direction. The schedule conflicts you mentioned were also a concern, and honestly the group dynamic with our existing cast felt important too."
